  first session clone /xfwm4/default back over the unbinding. xfconf merges per
  property, so commands/ and /xfwm4/default from libxfce4ui stay. Other
  workspace-switch shortcuts are unchanged. includes.chroot copies of both shortcut
  files match the package.
  xfce4-desktop.xml workspace0/workspace1 wallpaper slots are unchanged. No pager
  plugin is in the shipped panel. wrap_workspaces and scroll_workspaces are not
  set by this package.

  xfwm4.xml, plus xfce4-keyboard-shortcuts.xml under /etc/xdg/lcos and skel.
