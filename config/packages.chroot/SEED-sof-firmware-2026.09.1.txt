Purpose: Intel Panther Lake (ptl) and Wildcat Lake (wcl) laptop audio. LLK 7.2.6 (sound/soc/sof/intel/pci-ptl.c) asks for
intel/sof-ipc4/{ptl,wcl}/sof-{ptl,wcl}.ri, intel/sof-ipc4-lib/{ptl,wcl}/ and the sof-ptl-*/sof-wcl-* topologies in
intel/sof-ipc4-tplg/. excalibur's 2025.01-1 has none of them, so those laptops had no internal speakers or mics.
Also new: nvl, nvl-s (Nova Lake) firmware, more arl/lnl/mtl topologies, SDCA/UAOL/HDMI generic topologies.
